WRITING STYLE

==================================================

Write like a clear human operator.

Writing direction:

FLOW → HANDLING → RESULT

Never default to:

PAGES → DESIGN → FEATURES

Start with what the reader experiences, not what MindWP offers.

Good writing examples:

Leads come in. Nobody follows up.

Someone fills in the form. It lands in the wrong inbox.

The phone rings while your team is on a job. Nobody calls back.

People search nearby. They find the business with clearer services, stronger reviews, and a complete profile.

Bad writing examples:

We build high-converting websites.

We offer comprehensive digital solutions.

Our platform streamlines your lead management workflow.

Avoid SaaS, agency, and hype language.

Avoid words unless truly necessary:

workflow

pipeline

optimization

leverage

streamline

seamless

robust

scalable

solution

empower

cutting-edge

powerful

comprehensive

holistic

touchpoint

actionable insights

Allowed:

system, when it means MindWP’s actual named systems or clear business structure.

Public copy should be:

- plain English

- observable

- grounded

- direct

- slightly imperfect

- not over-polished

- not generic SaaS copy

Every important line should describe something visible, audible, measurable, or recognisable.

No fake metrics.

No guaranteed rankings.

No exaggerated outcomes.

No “skyrocket leads.”

No “dominate competitors.”

No “explosive growth.”

CTAs should feel like a conversation, not a pitch.

CTA tone examples:

Find where work is slipping

Do not use:

Book a free strategy call

Unlock growth

Claim your audit
