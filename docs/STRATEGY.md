# STRATEGY — MindWP

The commercial strategy. **Why** MindWP exists as a business, **who** it sells to, **what** it sells, **why anyone buys it over another web designer**, and **how leads actually arrive.** Every page exists to serve this doc.

This is the doc that was missing. The previous site was beautiful but sold a philosophy, not a service — it had no proof, buried the offer, and gave the buyer one soft next step. STRATEGY fixes that at the source. Identity lives in [FOUNDATION.md](./FOUNDATION.md); the offer structure in [OFFER-ARCHITECTURE.md](./OFFER-ARCHITECTURE.md); the look in [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md); page roles in [PAGES.md](./PAGES.md).

## The business in one line

MindWP sells **smart websites** — a website built to *win and handle* work — to established service businesses and specialist clinics, anywhere English is spoken. It is run by a builder who has been shipping WordPress sites since 2015, with a small senior team.

This is a deliberate move **off Fiverr-style design-to-WordPress conversion** and toward a productized authority business. The Fiverr work proves the craft; the website has to sell the *system*, not the conversion service.

## Market

**Global English.** Buyers are in the US, UK, and other English markets. Consequences for every page:

- Language is **market-neutral** — no US-only or UK-only spelling, currency, or local-authority references baked into core copy. Say "local area," not "your county" or "your council."
- Local SEO is told as a **concept** ("nearby customers find and verify you"), not as a US-map-pack or UK-specific tactic.
- Pricing posture uses neutral framing ("fixed-scope," "from a clear starting point") rather than a single currency where possible; if a number appears, it is clearly framed.
- Industry taxonomy spans both markets (e.g. Home Services includes roofing, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, foundation repair, septic, tree service — recognisable across markets).

## What we actually sell (the concrete offer)

The public product is the **Smart Website System**: a website *with the lead handling built in*. Concretely, a buyer gets a site that:

1. **Makes the offer clear and builds trust** before anyone calls (clear service/treatment pages, proof placed where decisions happen).
2. **Captures the enquiry** — calls, forms, messages, quote and consultation requests land somewhere useful, not in a forgotten inbox.
3. **Responds fast** — missed calls and after-hours enquiries move into a response path instead of disappearing.
4. **Owns the follow-up** — every enquiry has an owner, a status, and a next step; nothing rides on memory.
5. **Turns good work into proof** — review requests happen at the right moment; completed work becomes visible trust.

That bundle is delivered on a connected backend platform (kept **unnamed** in all public copy — never named, never the hero). The buyer is buying outcomes, not software.

**This is the line that must land by section 3 of the homepage and on every service page: a MindWP website doesn't just look good — it brings work in and makes sure it doesn't slip away.**

## Why a buyer chooses MindWP over any other web designer

The differentiator is **the handling is built in, not bolted on.** Every other web designer hands over a brochure and leaves; the enquiries are the owner's problem. MindWP's website is wired into response, ownership, follow-up, and reviews from day one. Said plainly to the buyer:

> Most websites stop at the contact form. Ours carries the enquiry the whole way through — answered, owned, followed up, and turned into proof.

Secondary differentiators: a real builder with a decade of craft (not a faceless agency or a $5 gig), honest fit filtering (we say who it's *not* for), and a calm diagnostic posture (we review before we pitch).

## Proof model (the biggest lever)

A brand nobody knows converts on **evidence**, and the old site had none. We have *some* real work to show (screenshots, anonymised where needed). The proof stack, strongest first:

1. **Demonstration builds** — finished, real example sites MindWP creates for each front-door vertical (roofing, dental implant, etc.). Real craft, shown big. These are the centrepiece of the "Work" section and the Work page. They are genuine builds, presented as demonstrations — never captioned as client results.
2. **Real client work** — anonymised screenshots/before-afters where client names can't be used; named where permission exists. Shown as visible structure and craft, not invented outcomes.
3. **Founder authority** — "shipping service-business websites since 2015," a small senior team. Real, modest, verifiable claims. No faces required.
4. **Process as proof** — showing *how* we work (the review, the build, the handling setup) is itself credibility.

**Hard line (unchanged from the old rules, and right):** no fake ratings, review counts, phone numbers, testimonials, client names, metrics, rankings, or guarantees. Authority comes from real work and real craft, never invented numbers. The fix for "no proof" is **show real work**, not **fabricate proof**.

## Conversion model

The old site had one soft CTA. The new model gives the buyer a clear offer, real proof, and **more than one way in**:

- **Primary CTA — "Request a Website Review":** the diagnostic entry point. We look at the current site + handling path and show where work is slipping. Low-pressure, high-value.
- **Secondary CTA — "See the work" / "See a sample review":** a lower-commitment path for buyers not ready to talk — routes to the Work page or a sample diagnostic.
- **Commercial signal:** a quiet pricing/scope posture (e.g. "fixed-scope, from a clear starting point") so buyers self-qualify on budget without a wall of tiers. The exact posture is settled in [PAGES.md](./PAGES.md); a single quiet line on the homepage CTA is enough.

Conversion sequence on any page: **show the buyer's situation → name what's slipping → state what the smart website changes → show real proof → invite the review.** Never escalate to the strong CTA before the buyer understands why it matters.

## Industry strategy

Two lanes, expanding over time. We cannot be sharp at 16 verticals on day one, so we **launch focused**.

### Lanes

- **Home Services** — roofing, HVAC, plumbing, foundation repair, septic services, tree service. Working reality: calls while on jobs, quote follow-up, storm/seasonal urgency, local trust, missed response.
- **Healthcare Practices** — dental implant, orthodontic, oral surgery, dermatology, ENT/sinus, podiatry, hearing aid/audiology, physiotherapy, optometry, orthopedic clinics. The website is the **practice front door**: patient trust, treatment clarity, consultation requests, booking confidence, follow-up. Never medical software, EMR, compliance, or treatment-claim pages.

### Front doors (build deep first)

- **Roofing** (Home Services) — high job value, storm-driven urgency, intense quote follow-up. A strong lead-gen vertical.
- **Dental Implant Clinics** (Healthcare) — very high case value, consultation-led, big decision friction. The premium clinic front door.

These two pages are built to the highest depth and double as the demonstration-build showcases. The two **lane hub pages** (Home Services, Healthcare Practices) are built next. The remaining 14 verticals are built from a **strong shared template** — real and specific, but lighter — and expanded over time. (Specific ≠ generic copy with the industry name swapped in; each must show that vertical's real timing, behaviour, and trust concerns — see [WRITING.md](./WRITING.md).)

## How leads actually arrive (lead-gen)

The homepage builds authority; it rarely ranks for buying intent. Leads come from:

1. **Industry pages** — buyers search "[industry] website" / "website for [practice type]." These are sharp lead-gen landing pages anchored by a demonstration build, not philosophy translations.
2. **Implementation pages** — tool-aware buyers search "Elementor / Divi / Bricks / WooCommerce / WordPress developer" or "website rebuild." These capture intent and move the buyer up to the Smart Website System. The tool is the route, not the value.
3. **The Work page** — proof that converts referral and comparison traffic.
4. **Resources/Blog** — top-of-funnel discovery that routes toward industry and service pages.

Implementation and industry pages are therefore **commercial priorities**, not afterthoughts.
